The handovers I own

Owning a layer means owning what crosses its edges, even when another team does the work.

Bring-up hands over inventory, firmware baseline, measured boot, DPU and network config, and machine-validation results. I agree that contract up front, so nothing gets tested twice.

In my PoC: Verda handed over H200s with ECC off, so the DCGM diagnostic skipped its memory test. A contract catches that on day one.

NVCRE certification passes, the staging taint comes off, and the node joins the production pool.

In my PoC: NVCRE certified the 2× H200 node at 351 GB/s all-reduce bus bandwidth over NVLink, then the node left staging.

Then NICo takes over below the line: reset, reflash, or wipe and re-isolate. The node comes back, I re-certify it, and gate 2 promotes it.

In my PoC: NVSentinel cut the outage from 360.9 s to 288.8 s and lifted goodput from 66.4% to 73.0%. Reboot and driver rebuild made up 82% of what was left.

NCCL bandwidth drops or links flap. I bring the evidence, the fabric team fixes it.
